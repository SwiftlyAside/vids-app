vids Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-03

Who uses vids

vids is a private tool used by a single operator to manage the operator's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public, and only the operator signs in to it.

Data accessed

With the Google account owner's consent, vids requests these scopes:

  • youtube.readonly — channel and video information of the signed-in account's channels;
  • yt-analytics.readonly — YouTube Analytics reports for those channels;
  • youtube.upload — uploading videos to those channels.

How data is used

The data is used only to upload the operator's own videos as private and to monitor the performance of the operator's own channels. vids does not use the data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it with third parties.

vids's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens and retrieved reports are stored only on the operator's own computer, outside any public location. Tokens are deleted when access is revoked or the tool is retired. Reports are kept only as long as they are needed for channel management.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions. See also the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
